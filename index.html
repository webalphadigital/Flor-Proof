Every bouquet with its story

Flor Proof is a platform where growers register their harvest and get a QR code. Customers scan it and know where their flowers come from.

Go to the forms

The problem

  • Small and medium flower growers keep track of their harvests without a reliable method.
  • Whoever buys the bouquet doesn't know who grew it or where it came from.
  • Without a brand, growers can't build a reputation or keep customers coming back.

The solution

Growers register each batch and get a QR code. It goes on the bouquet, and the customer scans it with their phone.

  1. The grower declares a batchVariety, zone, season, quantity, harvest date and shelf life.
  2. The florist confirms receiptRecords that the batch arrived and makes the bouquets.
  3. The customer scans the QRSees who grew it, where it comes from, which variety it is and how long it lasts.

Benefits

For the grower

Keep an organized record of every harvest, build your own brand and earn the reputation that brings customers back.

For the florist

Know where what you sell comes from and show it on every bouquet.

For the customer

Know which flowers you are buying: where they were grown, who grew them, which variety they are and how long they last.
